# Results

Generated by `make results` on Apple M1, 8 cores, Darwin 27.0.0.

## Data (`make data`)

50 symbol-days of synthetic order flow in LOBSTER format: 11,938,435 order book events across 10 symbols.

11,480 headlines scored by FinBERT (open source); FinBERT's sign agrees with the human label on 85.5% of bullish and bearish headlines (`make headlines`).

## Parameter sweep (`make sweep`)

10,240 parameter sets, 102,400 backtests on Spark in 14 minutes. The selected set has a per-minute Sharpe ratio of 0.761 against an expected maximum of 3.294 for 10,240 unskilled trials; deflated Sharpe ratio 0.000. The trial Sharpe ratios are spread widely because aggressive settings lose heavily, so the deflation sets a bar no trial reached: one half-hour selection window cannot prove skill on its own, which is why the comparison below uses untouched days.

## Held-out days (`make evaluate`)

Days 2026-10-22, 2026-10-23, all symbols, parameters fixed before these days were used.

| Strategy | P&L | Sharpe (annualized from 1-min P&L) | 10 s markout (ticks) | Fills | Max drawdown |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| baseline | $183.00 | 14.26 | 0.304 | 612 | $46.00 |
| signals_off | -$6,085.50 | -132.93 | -0.583 | 10,809 | $6,371.00 |
| signal | $10,137.00 | 236.43 | 0.613 | 14,311 | $82.50 |
| signal_optimized | $9,939.00 | 236.74 | 0.608 | 14,175 | $82.50 |

Switching the signals on, with every other parameter fixed, moves held-out P&L from -$6,085.50 to $10,137.00 and the 10-second markout from -0.583 to +0.613 ticks per share: without the forecast the strategy is picked off by informed flow, with it the fills move in its favour.

The synthetic market is built so that order flow leans toward a latent fair value, which makes it more predictable than a real one. The absolute P&L and Sharpe figures describe this simulation; the comparison between strategies on the same days is the meaningful result.

Annualizing intraday Sharpe ratios overstates what a full year would show; the figures are for comparing strategies on the same days, not as a forecast.
